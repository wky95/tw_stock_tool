# Provider evaluation and license questionnaire

Use the machine-readable templates in `docs/readiness/`. Record `unknown` rather than inferring an
answer. Public marketing is not a substitute for a contract term.

## Technical evaluation

For every candidate, identify the exact product/version, official or contractual source and review
date. Measure required-field and record coverage, announcement/revision timestamps, identifier
history, listing boundaries, corporate actions, tradability and exceptions, emergency calendar,
benchmark/market-cap PIT history, stream latency/sequence/recovery, correction/deletion notices and
target-host support. Record the longest verified history and explicit SLA. Do not rank incomparable
unknowns.

## License and operations

Provider and legal owners must answer whether non-display research/risk/trading use, real-time and
historical use, raw/normalized storage, encrypted backup/restore, derived artifacts, audit retention,
local display/export and any redistribution are permitted. Also capture access limits, regions,
subprocessors, incident notice, termination export, deletion deadlines, backup expiry and deletion
certification.

TWSE's official information pages state that covered applicants execute the appropriate agreement
and pay applicable fees, and that delayed information is 20 minutes or more behind real time. TWSE
also distinguishes own use from transmission to others. Those public statements do not answer this
system's exact non-display, backup, derived-use or retention rights; written review remains required.

## Acceptance decision

A candidate is acceptable only when every critical requirement has evidence, all license answers
needed by the deployment are affirmative or explicitly satisfied conditions, PIT record and field
coverage are 100%, critical disagreements are zero, and rollback remains licensed. Cost, commercial
terms and procurement approval are operator decisions outside this milestone.

The version 2 templates deliberately retain `UNKNOWN` for every unanswered provider, legal and
operator field. They include exact product/schema/entitlement/config versions, evidence
retrieval/review/expiry, numeric latency, history start/depth, all required reference/tradability/
benchmark/stream behaviors, target-process rights and termination deletion certification. Do not
change an answer to affirmative without referenced official or contract evidence.
